About Me

A deeper look into my journey, my skills, and my passion for cybersecurity.

Who I Am

I’m Juan Chavez, a Computer Science and Engineeringstudent at UC Merced (Class of 2027) with a strong focus on cybersecurity, databases, networks, operating systems, and cryptography. I’m passionate about understanding how systems work at a deep level — and how to secure them.

My cybersecurity journey accelerated through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate, where I completed hands-on labs involving risk assessments, Linux hardening, log analysis, SIEM tools, SQL filtering, and incident response. These labs helped me develop real-world skills that I now apply to personal projects and security research. In the projects section of this portfolio, you can find labs that I completed as part of the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate, as well as labs that I completed as part of my coursework at UC Merced.

Why Cybersecurity

I enjoy cybersecurity because it blends problem-solving, creativity, and technical depth. Every challenge is a puzzle — whether it’s analyzing suspicious logs, identifying vulnerabilities, or strengthening system defenses. I’m driven by the idea of protecting people, systems, and data in a world where threats evolve every day.
