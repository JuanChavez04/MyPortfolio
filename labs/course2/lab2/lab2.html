Lab 2 – Controls and Compliance

Course 2 - Play It Safe: Manage Security Risks

Overview

This lab focused on evaluating the security posture of Botium Toys by reviewing their assets, identifying risks, and completing a controls and compliance checklist. I analyzed administrative, technical, and physical controls, assessed gaps in compliance with PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC frameworks, and determined which controls were missing or insufficient. This activity strengthened my understanding of how organizations assess risk and implement controls to protect systems, data, and operations.

Tools Used

  • Risk assessment frameworks
  • NIST CSF Identify function
  • Controls categories (administrative, technical, physical)
  • Compliance frameworks (PCI DSS, GDPR, SOC 1 & 2)
  • Documentation review and analysis

What I Learned

  • How to evaluate an organization’s assets and determine which controls are currently in place.
  • How to identify missing or weak controls across administrative, technical, and physical categories.
  • How to assess compliance with major regulations such as PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC
  • How to assign a risk score based on missing controls, asset exposure, and regulatory gaps.
  • How to justify each control decision using evidence from the organization’s environment.
  • How to summarize findings and provide recommendations to improve security posture.

Scenario

Botium Toys is undergoing a security audit to evaluate its current controls, compliance posture, and overall risk level. The scope includes all assets managed by the IT department, including employee devices, internal systems, network infrastructure, storefront equipment, and data storage. The goal is to determine which controls are implemented, which are missing, and how these gaps impact the organization’s risk level and compliance with industry standards.

My Analysis

I reviewed Botium Toys’ assets and compared them against the control categories and compliance requirements. I identified several strong controls already in place, such as firewalls, antivirus software, CCTV, encryption, and physical locks. However, I also found significant gaps, including the absence of an intrusion detection system (IDS), no disaster recovery plan, weak password management practices, and inconsistent monitoring of legacy systems.

Using the provided frameworks, I completed the controls and compliance checklist, marking each control as “yes” or “no” and providing justification. I also evaluated the organization’s adherence to PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC requirements. Based on the missing controls and compliance gaps, I assigned a high risk score of 8 out of 10, indicating that Botium Toys needs to implement additional controls to reduce risk and strengthen its security posture.
