Lab 3 – Research Network Protocol Analyzers

Course 6 - Sound the Alarm: Detection and Response

Overview

In this activity, I compared two essential network analysis tools: Wireshark and tcpdump. Both tools capture and analyze network packets, but they serve different purposes depending on the environment and the level of detail required. This comparison highlights their strengths, differences, and when each tool is most effective.
